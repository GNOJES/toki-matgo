Toki Matgo social preview illustration (2026-10-03)

The rabbit, poster lettering and ink-wash background were generated with
Codex's built-in image generation tool for Toki Matgo. The depicted hwatu
cards were generated using the following artwork as visual references:

Set of Hwatu by Marcus Richert, published February 22, 2021.
Source: https://www.marcusrichert.com/images/hwatu/
Based on Hanafuda graphics by Louie Mantia, Jr. (http://louiemantia.com/).
Original contributions: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Contributions/Louiemantia

Changes: ink-wash reinterpretation of card motifs in an illustrated rabbit
poster, addition of Korean title and subtitle, resize to 1200x630 JPEG.
