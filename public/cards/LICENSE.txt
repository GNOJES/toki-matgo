Marcus Richert changed coloration and some linework to resemble a typical
Korean hwatu deck. Toki Matgo additionally serializes the SVG, crops each
card from the master, flattens transparent areas onto white, resizes to
256x417 and converts to lossless WebP. The card artwork has not been redrawn.
